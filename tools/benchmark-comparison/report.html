How to read these results

  • Change = (lookahead ÷ baseline − 1) × 100. Time saved = baseline − lookahead. Positive time saved means faster; negative means slower.
  • Summary cards cover all comparable scenarios for the chosen metric and statistic, independent of search and filters. The geometric mean gives each scenario equal weight; it is not a total workflow time or a claim of statistical significance.
  • Median is the default. Mean is also available to expose sensitivity to slow runs. Ranges and sample standard deviations describe observed variability, not confidence intervals. Run numbers are not paired experimental observations.
  • Cache order: Gradle User Home / Gradle Daemon / IntelliJ caches. Project caches are cleared alongside cold IntelliJ caches, per performance.scenarios. Script-change scenarios start with all three warm.
  • Components: Total, Gradle, and IDE times are taken directly from the profiler. Independently computed component medians need not sum to the total median.
  • Provenance: Values come from the structured benchmarkResult embedded in each original HTML report, matched by scenario and metric names. The CSV exports have unescaped commas in titles and collapsed empty fields; they are not used. Missing samples are never treated as zero.
  • Limits: These runs alone do not isolate the effect of the lookahead change from other snapshot differences, run order, or machine state. Repeat the experiment before treating small changes or variable regressions as conclusive.
